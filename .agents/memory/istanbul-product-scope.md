name: Istanbul product scope
description: Citywide coverage, branding constraints, and the distinction between samples and verified parking data.
---

The product covers all 39 Istanbul districts on both European and Asian sides, despite retaining the Fatih Park brand. Geographic expansion must not redesign existing controls, phone bottom sheets, tablet panels, or car mode.

**Why:** The user explicitly expanded Fatih-only coverage while asking to keep the visual design and all other functionality unchanged.

**How to apply:** Do not infer a Fatih-only scope from the app name. Keep future geographic/data changes separate from visual redesigns. Sample lot names, locations, occupancy and prices are fictional demonstrations, not verified facilities; do not represent them as real İSPARK data. Actual provider imports require reviewed source records rather than invented missing values.
